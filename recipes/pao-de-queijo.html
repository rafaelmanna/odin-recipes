Pão de Queijo

Brazilian Cheese Bread

Description

Pão de Queijo is a traditional Brazilian cheese bread made with tapioca flour and cheese. It has a crispy outside and a soft, chewy inside. These small cheese rolls are naturally gluten-free and are very popular for breakfast or snacks in Brazil.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 cups tapioca flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Heat the milk, oil, and salt in a saucepan.
  3. Pour the hot mixture over the tapioca flour and mix well.
  4. Add the eggs and cheese.
  5. Mix until a smooth dough forms.
  6. Roll the dough into small balls.
  7. Bake for 20 minutes or until golden.

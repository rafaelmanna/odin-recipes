Coxinha

Brazilian Chicken Croquettes

Description

Coxinha is a famous Brazilian street food snack filled with shredded chicken and covered in a crispy golden crust. Its shape looks similar to a chicken drumstick, and it is commonly found in bakeries and snack bars across Brazil.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups shredded chicken
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • Salt and pepper
  • Breadcrumbs
  • Oil for frying

Steps

  1. Cook the onion with butter in a saucepan.
  2. Add the chicken broth and bring to a boil.
  3. Add the flour and stir until a dough forms.
  4. Let the dough cool slightly.
  5. Flatten small portions of dough and fill with shredded chicken.
  6. Shape into teardrop forms.
  7. Cover with breadcrumbs and fry until golden brown.
